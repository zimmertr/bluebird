# 0124. The wind gust is one option of the Wind row

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Decider: the maintainer, on #584 and its PR #685 (2026-10-08: rank the gust; 2026-10-09, reviewing the first build: fold it into the Wind row, because two ranking rows for wind and wind gust read as two unrelated measurements)
- Issues and PRs: #584, #685, #257, #295, #678
- Cited in code as: #584
- Guide: [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md), the paragraph after "No column header names the method" and The Python and TypeScript mirrors (row 35); [`backend/CLAUDE.md`](../../backend/CLAUDE.md), the `app/services/aggregation.py` bullet; [`frontend/src/utils/CLAUDE.md`](../../frontend/src/utils/CLAUDE.md), the `colors.ts`, `tableColumns.ts`, `chartData.ts`, `popupRows.ts`, `constraints.ts` and `unavailableCell.ts` bullets

## Context

The app had no gust data. Every wind number was the sustained wind, interpolated to the destination's elevation ([0022](0022-wind-at-elevation.md)), and the Metrics table's wind ceiling was hinted as "The gustiest hour must be at most this." although it read the windiest sustained hour. For a ridge or a summit the gust is the number that turns a party around, and Open-Meteo serves it as `wind_gusts_10m` on the same request the app already makes. #584 asked whether to add it as a fourth wind aggregate, a column with no ranking, or a ranked metric of its own. The first build was a ranked family of its own, a `Wind gust` row in the Metrics table with its own Min, Max and Avg; the maintainer reviewed it on the preview and reversed the shape.

## Decision

1. Request `wind_gusts_10m` in the one hourly weather request, on both the pod and the browser, and rank, bound, colour, chart and play it back.
2. It is one option of the Wind row, not a family of its own. The key is `wind_gust_mph`, ONE number per destination: the strongest gust in the window, at the wind's one decimal. A least or an average gust is not a number anyone plans against. The Wind row's dropdown reads Avg, Gust, Max, Min, alphabetical by the word each option shows, and the Wind row's default stays `wind_avg_mph`. `aggregateToken` reads `gust` out of the key and `AGGREGATE.gust` is the word `Gust`.
3. The Wind row's ceiling box limits the gust (`maxWindGustMph` ↔ `max_wind_gust_mph`, a new request field with no floor partner) under main's hint, "The gustiest hour must be at most this.", which is now true. The floor stays the calmest sustained hour. `max_wind_mph` keeps its sustained meaning for API callers. The link keeps the box's param, `maxwind`, because a bound's param names the control rather than the field it reads, so a link written before this change opens with its number in the same box, now read against the gust.
4. A gust ranking's heading is `Highest Wind gust` / `Lowest Wind gust` in a window and at one hour alike, through one constant, `WIND_GUST`, rather than the general rule's `Gust Wind`.
5. The table and the file carry one column, `Wind · Gust (mph)`, last in the wind's group, linked to Windy's `gust` overlay at the hour of the strongest gust. It keeps its column and its word in a point sample, since that hour's gust is not the hour's wind.
6. The popup gives the gust a line of its own, `Wind gust (mph)`, after `Wind (mph)` where the A to Z sort puts it, with its one number in the Max column and the other cells empty. A gust ranking marks that line and never the wind's.
7. The chart has no gust entry. Under Wind every line, compared models' lines included, gains a dashed twin in its own colour reading the gust (`CHART_GUST_DASH`, `8 4`, longer than the seams' `4 3` and the grid's `3 3`). It is not faded, because 0.25 opacity already means dimmed by hover. Hovering either line lifts and dims the pair together, the y range covers the gust, and the hover box lists `{name} (gust)` directly under its wind line.
8. The colour scale is the wind's verdict ramp on [15, 25, 35, 46, 58] mph, a per-key scale like the rate columns' rather than a family's. 46 and 58 are the National Weather Service's Wind Advisory and High Wind Warning gust criteria; the wind's own 5/15/25/35/50 would paint nearly every gust orange or worse. Markers, the legend, the forecast grid and playback read it, and playback reads the hourly gust series.
9. The gust is the 10 m value as served and is not adjusted to elevation, so `heightDependentKey` leaves it out although the wind is a height family, and no header names its height ([0051](0051-no-method-in-headers.md)). It is reduced outside the precipitation, temperature and wind zip, as the freezing level is ([0026](0026-freezing-level-outside-zip.md)), and is null on its own. Its declared unit must read `mp/h`.

## Evidence

- Measured 2026-10-08 at the Rainier summit (46.8523, -121.7603), `forecast_days=3`, `wind_speed_unit=mph`. Hours with a gust, then the 10 m wind average, the gust average and the gust maximum in mph: GFS Seamless 72 of 72, 11.6, 18.8, 49.9; GEM 72, 6.6, 8.3, 44.1; ECMWF IFS 0.25° 72, 4.0, 14.3, 33.8; HRRR 67 of 72, 12.2, 20.1, 49.9; UK Met Office 72, 3.7, 9.2, 25.5; ICON 72, 5.3, 34.6, 78.1; JMA 0 of 72 under the unit `undefined`, wind 6.3; Météo-France 72, 3.1, 12.0, 31.3. The archive (2026-07-01 to 07-03) answered 72 of 72 in `mp/h`, average 24.5, maximum 31.5. `docs/DATA.md` has the table.
- `wind_gusts_925hPa` answers HTTP 400 (2026-10-08), so there is no level gust to interpolate at a summit's height.
- Windy's gust overlay token, read off Windy's index.js v51.3.2 on 2026-10-08: `ident: 'gust'`, shown as "Wind gusts".
- The weight: one more variable in a request already over the floor, so the factor moves from 1.5 to 1.6 on the pod (sixteen variables) and from 1.6 to 1.7 in the browser (seventeen). A 50-location 16-day batch costs 91.4 weighted calls on the pod, where it cost 85.7, and 97.1 in the browser. Folding the gust into the Wind row changed what is shown, not what is fetched, so these stand.
- The chart's cost, measured 2026-10-09 in the built image under the pinned Playwright Chromium, over a 200-row report: the synchronous React work of switching the chart to Wind went from a median of 48 and 50 ms (the separate-family build, 200 lines) to 112 and 108 ms (400 lines), and switching back to Temperature from 46 and 49 ms to 103 and 105 ms. The same build with the dash removed measured 113 and 106 ms, so the cost is the doubled line count and not the dash. It is paid once per switch of the chart's metric, not per hover.

## Alternatives rejected

- A ranked family of its own, a `Wind gust` row with its own Min, Max and Avg, its own bound boxes and its own chart entry: built first, and reversed by the maintainer on review (2026-10-09), because two ranking rows for wind and wind gust read as two unrelated measurements. It also offered a least and an average gust nobody plans against.
- A column with no ranking: everything else the table shows ranks, and a column that cannot be asked about would be the one exception.
- Interpolating a gust to the destination's elevation from the level winds: there is no level gust, and a ratio applied to the level wind would be a number no model produced.
- A separate chart entry for the gust: the gust is read against the wind it rides on, so the two belong on one plot.

## Consequences

`weather_vectors.json` pins the gust's reduction on both sides (mirror row 35), and `mirrored_constants.json` moves `N_VARIABLES` to 16. The pod's capacity figures move with the weight factor, measured 2026-10-08 through `_check_pacing` with the cloud batches every analysis now prices ([0121](0121-cloud-deck-fetched-on-every-analysis.md)): an unkeyed archive analysis is first refused at 30 days, where it was 31, and the most destinations it may take falls from 249 to 248 at 31 days, from 150 at 61 days to 150 at 59, from 99 at 62 to 99 at 60, and to 26 at 357 days, where it was 28 at 355. The 16-day forecast window still books its last batch at the pacer's 120 s wait bound and does not pass it, so 1,500 destinations stay allowed whole. A stored column set from before this change (`columns8` and older) migrates with the gust column shown, under the new key `columns9`. A link with `maxwind` from before the change now bounds the gust, which is stricter than the sustained ceiling it used to set. Under Wind the chart draws twice the lines, so the hover's point budget (`tracksCursor`) is spent twice as fast there. JMA's rows read `N/A` in the gust column, in the table, the popup and the file, with no note on hover; ranking by the gust under JMA orders nothing, and the model comparison has no check for a gust ranking that compares JMA, where it blocks Analyze for a freezing-level ranking that compares a model without one. Either would need a sentence the maintainer has not approved.
