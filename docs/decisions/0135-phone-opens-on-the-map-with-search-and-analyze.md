## Context

On a phone, finding the forecast for one named place took five steps across three surfaces (TJ, on an Android phone, 2026-10-08). The drawer opened over the map at every width, so the search field the Destinations section named was under it. A picked place was framed for a map with no sheet, and the sheet then opened over its label; a reader whose saved mode was Both met an empty chart frame over a single row of dashes. And the only Analyze was back inside the drawer they had just closed, with the cue that said so beside it.

## Decision

- **A phone opens on the map, a shared link included.** The drawer starts closed below the desktop breakpoint and open above it (`useDrawer`), read once at mount. A desktop is unchanged.
- **The search field spans the map's top edge on a phone, with two rows under it on one grid of two tracks, `4fr` and `3fr`** (`MAP_ROW_GRID`): Controls beside Layers, then the Legends card under Controls beside Analyze under Layers. Before a report there is no legend, and Analyze stands alone under Layers. Every button takes its width from its track and none states its own, so the ratio holds at every width: Controls and Legends 190, 207 and 230px at 360, 390 and 430 wide, Layers and Analyze 142, 155 and 172. The legend stack stands on the same tracks, which is what puts the card at exactly Controls' width. Four to three because Controls carries a word and a summary where the others carry a word. Controls shows its word and a line under it composed of two words the panel already shows, the When arm and the model's label, `Current · NOAA GFS` (`controlsSummary`; the arm words are spelled once, in `WHEN_ARM_LABEL`, which the calendar reads). It is on the grid whether or not the drawer is open, so nothing reflows under a closing drawer. The desktop's column, and its Controls standing in it only while the panel is closed, are unchanged.
- **The map's Analyze is the panel's command, not a second one.** It calls the same `handleAnalyze`, opens and closes on the same gate (`analyzeGateFor`, which the panel builds its gate with too), reads `Analyzing…` while a run is under way, and cancels and fails exactly as the panel's does. It never explains itself: every blocker and every commit cue stays under the panel's Analyze ([0028](0028-notices-below-analyze.md)). That is the accepted cost of a second Analyze.
- **A searched place is framed for the sheet it is about to open.** `useResultsLayout` publishes `sheetPadBottomPx`, the resting lift the sheet will take once it is shown, which reads nothing that depends on `showTable`; `MapStage` hands it to `flyToPlace`. The camera keeps using the RESTING lift, never the live one.
- **A phone sheet holding only pending rows shows the table alone** (`heldResultsMode`), without writing the reader's saved mode, which comes back with the first report. A press on the segment while the hold stands is the reader's answer and wins. A desktop is never held.
- **The vendor's stack starts below the rows on a phone** (`MAP_EDGE.belowRow`, read by `map.css`: 156px on a finger, the three rows and their gaps), and the legend stack hangs from the third row at the short column's height.
- **The map's own buttons on a phone** (`addControls` in `map/controls.ts`, given a coarse pointer, and `map.css`): no zoom buttons, since a pinch zooms; the compass stays at the top right; the locate button moves to the bottom right, directly above the attribution's (i), the two on one vertical axis, round, both at the 44px touch target (the (i) grows to it), 8px apart and 8px above the band's bottom. The attribution still opens to the left from the (i). A desktop keeps its zoom buttons and its stack.

## Evidence

- Measured 2026-10-13 at 390x844 on a coarse pointer against the built image: a pick of Mount Rainier put the marker at y 248 and its label at 272, above a sheet whose top edge stood at 496.
- The grid's fit, measured the same day in the Playwright image's Chromium (`docs/STYLES.md`, Phone row fit): every member 44px tall, the tracks 189.7 and 142.3px at 360, 206.8 and 155.2 at 390, 229.7 and 172.3 at 430. `Current · NOAA GFS` (93px at the 10px micro step) fits everywhere; `Current · Meteo-France ARPEGE` (148px) truncates at 360 only, and on a Mac, which draws the face up to 7% wider, both ARPEGE summaries truncate at 360 and fit at 390 with about 2px to spare.
- The locate button and the (i) at 390x844 with a report open: both 44px, both at x 336, the button at y 392 to 436 and the (i) at 444 to 488, 8px above a sheet whose top stood at 496.
- The column the grid replaced was three rows (search, Controls, Layers) and ended at 156px on a finger. The grid's second row ends at 104, and the legend card's header takes the third row, so the sheet rests where it did (`LEGEND_TOP_PX` is still 156).

## Alternatives rejected

- **Option A, the search field at the top of the drawer on a phone:** the fly-to would happen behind the drawer, out of sight, and the phone would carry two search fields feeding one `registerPlace`.
- **Option D, an analysis run by the search pick itself when nothing else waits:** it would narrow [0003](0003-analyze-is-spend-boundary.md), and the maintainer kept it whole: no analysis ever runs without a press of Analyze.
- **Option E, Analyze in the results bar:** out of place there, it costs the sheet's height, and it wraps the bar's second line.
- **Directions 2 and 3 of the round-one mockups**, one bottom sheet and a drawer with an action bar: too cluttered and confusing (the maintainer).
- **Placements B, C and D of round two:** B drops the summary that tells a new reader what Controls holds; C hides the one primary action behind a new glyph with no room for its reasons; D makes Controls and Layers icons, which record 0090 rules out for a control whose name a reader must see.
- **The five mocked row layouts, and the first build's single row of Controls, Layers and Analyze sized by their words:** rejected by the maintainer for the two-track grid above, whose widths come from the grid rather than from the words.
- **Fully rounded pills, as the mockups drew them:** the search field above and the legend under them are both the floating box, and one cluster in two radii read as two kinds of object.

## Consequences

`MapButtonColumn` takes the grid's props (`phoneRow`, `controlsSummary`, `analyzeEnabled`, `analyzing`, `onAnalyze`), `LayersPopover` an `inRow` flag, `MapLegend` an `onGrid` flag, `SearchBox` a `wide` flag, `MapView`'s `flyToPlace` an optional bottom padding, and `addControls` a `touch` flag. The new roles are `MAP_BUTTON`, `MAP_PILL`, `MAP_PILL_TEXT`, `MAP_PILL_SUMMARY`, `MAP_ANALYZE`, `MAP_ROW_GRID`, `MAP_ROW_W`, `MAP_EDGE.right` and `MAP_EDGE.belowRow`, and `MAP_COL_GAP_T` went with the Layers popover (record 0136), held in `styles.test.ts` and the `style-map-*` checks. Two Analyze buttons share a name on a phone, one inert inside the closed drawer, so a browser test there picks it through the main landmark. The panel's Map group still only rings the search field on hover; the handle `MapStage` holds on the field has no caller. A phone browser suite for the whole flow waits on #693.
