## Context

PR #708 and PR #733 gave the phone a map of its own: a search pill with the locate button at its right, and a row of chips under it. A desk kept the older column at the map's top left: a boxed search field, then Controls, Layers and Legend as 184px buttons, with the zoom pair and the compass in the library's top-right stack. One app looked like two.

## Decision

- **The desk's map wears the phone's two rows.** The pill stands alone on the first, 320px wide, and Legend and Layers on the chip row under it. The rows are as wide as the wider of the two (`lg:w-max`, no narrower than the pill), so the rest of the map's top stays the map's.
- **A desk's map buttons stand in the map's bottom-right corner, as Google Maps stands its own** (TJ, 2026-10-20): the credit line flush in the corner on the results' edge, with the scale at its end, and over it the compass, the locate button and the zoom pair, 24px in from the map's right edge, 6px apart and 10px above the line (`MapCorner`, `MAP_CORNER`). A click on + or − zooms where a mouse has no wheel. A phone keeps no zoom pair, because a pinch zooms there, and keeps the locate button at its pill and the compass at its chip row's end.
- **The zoom pair is one capsule** (`MAP_ZOOM_PAIR`): two 40px halves in the map button's chrome, the lower half's top edge the rule between them. Each half takes the raised fill under the pointer where the round buttons take the sky edge, because the rule is the lower half's border alone and could not light for the upper one.
- **The scale stands in a box as wide as its widest bar** (`SCALE_MAX_WIDTH_PX`), the bar at its right, so the credits keep their place on the line while the bar changes with the zoom. There it stands on the line rather than on the map, so it takes no scrim, and its ends are drawn in its label's ink in both themes: the dark theme's ends, drawn dark to stand on the light basemap, all but vanished on the dark line. The line keeps the library's 20px line (`leading-5`): the map's container gives it to the library's controls, and the corner stands outside that container, so without it the scale shrank to 17px from the 22px box record 0162 holds it to.
- **A desk's compass always shows.** While the map is north-up and flat it is disabled and greyed (`DISABLED`), so the column keeps one shape; a phone's hides then, as before. A compass that idles under the keyboard, as a press on it does, hands the keyboard to the map. A zoom half the library disables at either end of the zoom greys the same way.
- **Legend comes before Layers, at both widths.** The legend card is the one open by default, and first in the row it hangs over the map's left edge and covers less of the map (TJ, 2026-10-18).
- **The summary chip stands at the chip row's front only while the panel is shut**, since the docked panel has its own Close. It takes the keyboard when the panel closes under it.
- **Analyze stays in the panel**, with every notice under it as record 0028 says. A desk has no floating button, so closing the panel puts Analyze away. The maintainer accepted that.
- **Each chip's menu is a whole card 4px under its chip**, from the chip's left edge, 184px wide (`MAP_MENU_W`, `MAP_CHIP_MENU`). 4px is closer than the 8px between the pill and the chip row, so the card reads as its chip's menu. The chip stays a whole pill and is lit while its card is out, as a phone's is. The Layers menu stands over the legend card. Amended 2026-10-29, the maintainer's call on #799: the two cards no longer share a width. The legend card keeps one fixed width (`CHIP_CARD_W.key`, 224px since 0237), and the Layers menu is as wide as its widest row and never narrower than its chip (`CHIP_CARD_W.menu`), as every other menu is. A reader who opens one card over the other no longer sees one edge; that was judged the lesser cost.
- **A desk's chips carry a chevron; a phone's do not.** `IconChevron` follows the Legend and Layers labels at a desk (`MAP_CHIP_CHEVRON`), down while the card is shut and up while it is open, because the card drops from the chip. A phone's chip opens a sheet that rises from below, so the arrow would point the wrong way there (TJ, 2026-10-18).
- **The two cards wear one surface** (`SURFACE_CHIP_CARD`): the chips' colour, opaque where the chips stay at 95% (record 0201), and the structural slate-500 edge rather than the quiet frame. They are twins, identical chips each opening a card of one width, so two looks asked the reader to find a difference that is not there (TJ, 2026-10-18). Only the shadow differs: the legend rests on the map with the chips (`raised`), and the Layers menu opens over it (`overlay`), where its outline and deeper shadow part the two.
- **The legend card is as tall as its sections**, open until the reader folds it as record 0148 had it, and scrolls inside the map's height under the rows (`legendMaxCss`, read off the map wrapper as `100cqh`).

## Alternatives rejected

- **B, restyling the column in place:** two layouts for one app would remain.
- **C, a Material rail and app bar:** a rebuild of the panel for a map tool whose panel already works.
- **A1, a floating Analyze on the desk's map, and A3, an Analyze chip:** both pull Analyze away from the notices that explain it.
- **Menus joined to their chip**, record 0134's rule for every other menu: built first, it squared the pill's bottom corners into a dome (TJ, 2026-10-18). Of three mockups, a joined card under a dark chip and square-cornered chips that read as tabs lost to the whole lit pill with its card set apart.
- **An 8px gap under the chip**, the same as the one between the pill and the chip row: built, and the card read as a row of its own rather than as its chip's menu (TJ, 2026-10-18).
- **A chevron on a phone's chips too**: its sheet rises from below, and the two chevrons would cost the phone's chip row about 34px it does not have.
- **Seventeen other treatments** in the nineteen mockups of 2026-10-18: gaps of 0, 2 and 6px; a tonal chip, an unlit chip and an accent underline for the open state; a pointer notch, an accent edge on the card and a squared corner under the pill; the chip sitting on the card's top edge or inside its corner; and square-cornered chips, detached or joined as tabs. The accent says a thing acts, so an accent card edge spends it on decoration; a notch and a joined tab read as older patterns; and a chip on or inside its card reads as a frame from an animation.
- **Two surfaces for the two cards**, kept from the layout before: the legend on `SURFACE_FLOATING` and the Layers menu a step lighter on `SURFACE_POPOVER`, a key the reader reads and a menu the reader acts in. That held when the legend was a box in a column and Layers a popover off a button; beside each other under twin chips, the difference read as a mistake.
- **An accent outline on the cards**, option M of the nineteen: the legend card is open most of a session, so it would wear the colour that says a thing acts on a key the reader only reads.
- **No zoom buttons at a desk**, this record's answer of 2026-10-17: reversed on 2026-10-20 (TJ), because a click on + or − zooms where a mouse has no wheel, and Google Maps, Apple Maps and CalTopo all keep the pair at a desk.
- **The compass beside the locate button at the pill**, this record's answer of 2026-10-18: both left the pill for the corner with the zoom pair.
- **The library's top-right stack**, built first on 2026-10-20 and the first corner mockup: zoom in, zoom out, the compass and the locate button in the library's own order, 12px in and 4px apart, the first level with the pill.
- **Google Maps' arrangement on the app's own spacing**, the second: the same corner, 12px in and 4px apart, the zoom pair two round buttons.
- **The second with the legend as a fixed card at the map's bottom left**, the fourth, in place of the Legend chip: at 1024 with the panel open the card stood where the forecast player stands.
- **A card's lone button at its right**, Material's place for a card's action: built in this PR and reverted the next day (TJ, 2026-10-18), because the centred button reads cleaner.
- **A phone's credit line, the desk's mirrored into the bottom-left corner on the sheet's edge**, holding the (i) and the scale, with the Analyze button rising 16px over its opened credits. Built and measured on 2026-10-21 at 360x800 and 390x800, it stood 153 by 29px folded at both widths, 70.9px and 100.9px clear of the button, and opened to 348 and 378px wide and 73 tall with the button 16px over it. TJ reverted it the same day: "It was better before. Revert the change for mobile. We can have this drawn differently between mobile/desktop to accommodate for their unique resolutions/aspect ratios." A phone keeps its own foot by design, as record 0140 draws it.

## Consequences

`MapButtonColumn.tsx` is gone and `PhoneMapRows.tsx` became `MapRows.tsx`, rendered at both widths. `MapLegend` is the Legend chip at both widths, opening a card at a desk and a sheet on a phone. `SearchBox` has one look, the pill. `MAP_COL_W`, `MAP_ROW_H`, `MAP_COL_GAP` and `LEGEND_TOP` are gone from `styles.ts`, and so are `LIFTED_EDGE` and `SEGMENT_FLUID_LIFTED`, which existed for the Layers menu's lighter fill; `SURFACE_POPOVER` keeps the search results list, the coverage well takes `SLIDER.well`, and `RECESSED_FILL` is private to `styles.ts` like `RECESSED_EDGE`, and `IconLayers` and `IconLegend` from `icons.tsx`. `MAP_SLOT.besideLocate` is gone, a desk's rows hand up no slots, and `hideCompassWhileNorthUp` became `idleCompassWhileNorthUp`. `MapCorner.tsx` is new: `map/controls.ts` adds the compass and the zoom pair as two navigation controls where the library would draw one group, so the locate button can stand between them, and hands back the scale, which a desk's corner stands too; `placeControl` takes the library corner a control goes back to. `e2e/deskCorner.spec.ts` holds the corner's geometry in a browser. The lint checks that pinned the column became `style-map-chrome`, `style-map-rows`, `style-map-layers-chip` and `style-map-legend-chip`.
