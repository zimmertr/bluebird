## Context

#687's audit found two things in the Destinations section. The Coordinates block (its heading and a three-line textarea with example rows) was always open, 95px of the section's 281px before anyone had pasted anything (P06). And the Map sub-block was a subheading and an italic helper line, 48px with no control in it, because the search box it described is on the map (P07). The maintainer's rule for P07: the helper may go only if the panel still makes it obvious, without the tutorial, that searching adds a destination.

## Decision

- **The order is Map, Coordinates, Polygon**, so the two full-width inputs stand together under their subheadings and the Polygon block (its buttons, the type boxes, Include unnamed peaks) comes last.
- **Map is what main had**: its subheading and the helper line `Search by name, or click any peak or lake.`, with hovering the group pointing at the search box and the clickable peaks and lakes. A field in the panel that focused the map's search box was built here and reverted on the maintainer's third look (2026-10-13): on a desktop the map's own search box is always on screen beside it, so the panel's field was a second way to reach a box already in view, and the maintainer judged it worse than the line it replaced. How the panel should offer searching is the open question #710 holds.
- **Coordinates is a box two rows tall at rest**, so a reader sees that several rows go in, with the format line and one row that follows it as its placeholder (`# Lat,Lon or Lat,Lon,Name` and `46.8529,-121.7604,Mount Rainier`). The third example row is gone. It is the textarea it was, resizable by its corner, at two rows rather than three.
- **The Polygon row is always the same three buttons**: the mode button (`Draw polygon`, `Edit polygon` or `Done`), `Cancel` and `Clear`, a third of the width each (`DRAW_ROW`), so the coordinates box and the row stand on one pair of edges. A button that does nothing is greyed with `DISABLED` rather than missing: Cancel outside the mode, Clear with no point, Done under three points (`drawControls`). The buttons are the two button roles at a 4px inset, because a third is narrower than `Draw polygon` at the roles' 12px. A press that greys the button it landed on hands the keyboard to the first button in the row that can still act (#576, record 0089), and a press that leaves its button live keeps it there.
- **The polygon's area shares the counter's line**, under the `Polygon` subheading: the counter at the left, the area right-justified on its baseline, both in the colours they had (`STATUS.error` over the cap, the caption otherwise). Neither breaks inside itself; when the two do not fit side by side the area moves to a line of its own, still at the right. Nothing is shrunk to make them fit.

## Evidence

With a ring drawn and Peaks ticked it is 323 to 289px at 1440x900 and 396 to 362px at 390x844.
- The counter's line at 360px, measured at 12px in Chrome on a Mac: the line is 295px and the pair needs an 8px gap, so 287px for both. Outside draw mode it holds every ring under the cap: `99 points placed` (94.9px) and `~99,999 km²` (72.8px) are 175.7px. A ring just over the cap fits up to 9 points (`4 points placed` beside `~100,001 km² (max 100,000 km²)` is 281px) and not at 99 (288.6px). While drawing a closed ring the counter is the long one: `3 points placed. Press Done when ready.` is 229.8px and leaves 57.2px, which holds `~779 km²` (53.4px) and nothing of four digits (`~2,109 km²` is 63px); from 10 to 99 points it is 235.4 to 237.6px and leaves room for a two-digit area only (`~99 km²`, 47.3px). Past 99 points the counter alone is 243px and up (250.5px at the cap's thousand), which still fits the line, with the area on the next. So the pair shares the line outside the mode and, while drawing, only for a ring under 1,000 km²; at 1440x900 the line is 317px and the pair fits while drawing up to five-digit areas at 3 to 9 points (310.6px with the gap). The single wrap is the area moving down, which is why the area is the one with `ml-auto`.
- The row and the coordinates box share their edges: 16 to 333px at 1440x900, 16 to 341px at 390x844 and 16 to 311px at 360x800 (measured 2026-10-13). A third of the row is 100.3px, 103px and 93px there. `Draw polygon` is 77.4px on a Mac, so at the roles' 12px inset it needs 101.4px and wraps on a desktop and at 360px; at 4px it needs 85.4px and fits all three.
- `DestinationsSection.test.tsx` holds the order, the Map helper line, the area on the counter's line, both kept whole, the box's two rows and its placeholder, the row's three buttons in every state and where the keyboard goes after each press. `drawControls.test.ts` holds what each button says and whether it can act, case by case. `styles.test.ts` holds `DRAW_ROW` to the two roles and the fit.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Direction a, three labelled rows in a 72px label column** (round 2): every control kept and no word added, but it saved 16px on a desktop and 2px on a phone, and its search row stood apart from the coordinates.
- **A field in the panel for the map's search** (built in this PR, reverted on the maintainer's third look, 2026-10-13): a button drawn as an empty field with the search glyph and the line `Search by name, or select from the map`, which handed the keyboard to the map's search box and on a phone closed the drawer first. It took the helper line's place, but on a desktop the search box it pointed at is always in view beside it, so it was a second door to a room already open. #710 holds how the panel should offer searching.
- **Coordinates as one line until opened** (built in this PR, reversed on the maintainer's first look, 2026-10-13): a one-line button drawn as an empty field, in mono, showing the format line, which a press turned into the textarea in place, folding back when emptied and left. It saved 23px at 1440x900 and 14px on a phone over the two-row box, but one line did not show that several rows go in.
- **Coordinates as a closed disclosure** (round 1's P06): a chevron beside the heading hid the format until opened.
- **Only the buttons that can act** (the row's shape since #478): Draw polygon alone at rest, two over a ring, three while drawing. The row changed width under the reader and stood on no edge the box above it did.
- **The counter beside the subheading and the area under it**, the maintainer's first ask: it fits up to 99 points and wraps from 100, and nothing on that line may wrap at 360px.
- **The area beside the subheading and the counter under it** (built in this PR, moved on the maintainer's fourth look, 2026-10-13): it fit every case, and the maintainer moved the area to the counter's line, beside the reading it belongs with.
- **Removing the Map sub-block outright** (round 1's P07): searching would then be visible only on the map, which fails the maintainer's rule.

## Consequences

The Map group is what main had, so the search box's ref stays in `MapStage`. The Polygon row sits on the record 0040 exception for full-width controls, like the coordinates box and the calendar's field. Two buttons named Cancel can now be on screen together while an analysis runs, the analysis's own and the row's greyed one, so the browser suite presses the one that is enabled.
