# 0048. The map legend is one box, in alphabetical order, anchored at the top

Verbatim guide text at 971fede, copied before the edit to the template.

## From `frontend/src/CLAUDE.md`, line 74

**The map's legend is ONE box**, gaining and losing sections as layers toggle and as the ranking changes, the metric colour key among them (#454; it is not a layer — it explains the marker colours, which exist with every layer off — but it is a section of the same box, because a second box cost a border, a gap and a backdrop on a phone whose map can be 161px tall). Five separate boxes cost ~100px of that before #446, and merging the last two took the stack from 313px to 182px (measured 2026-09-17 at 402px, every layer on). **Every section reads in alphabetical order by the label it RENDERS**, the metric key included (TJ, 2026-09-17), so `Active wildfire` leads and a `Temperature` ranking sorts last where an `AQI` one sorts first; it is `.sort()`ed in `App.tsx` rather than written in order, because one of the labels is the ranked metric's and no source order can be both. Every section is built by the one `legendSection` in `App.tsx`, which is what lets them be sorted at all: a key on a single value is a ROW with its swatch on the right, and a key on a SCALE is a strip with `legendRamp.ts`' numbers drawn INSIDE it, along the bottom edge on a scrim. Inside rather than under because a strip and a row of numbers under it is two lines and a strip alone is one, which is 14px a section over two sections (TJ, 2026-09-17); the scrim is what makes the ink measurable, since no single colour clears AA over ramps that run the whole hue circle. **The stack is anchored at the TOP**, under the button that switches the layers it explains, and grows downward on both breakpoints (#249 review): a key belongs where the reader last looked for it, and anchored to the bottom it rode up and down with every panel drag while the last box ended up under the forecast player. The `bottom` offset it still carries is a ceiling on the scroll box rather than an anchor — it stops the stack above the timeline's band and above the sheet's top edge, so no box is ever half under a control — and the overflow therefore leaves through the BOTTOM, which is the edge a scroll can follow. Neither an auto margin on the first child nor `justify-end` may come back: a column pushed to the end edge overflows past its START edge, where content sits at negative coordinates with `scrollTop` pinned at 0 and is **unreachable**, measured at 402x874 with four of five boxes there; the linter's `map-stage-anchors` check fails either pattern.
