# 0270. A card's body stands between a quarter and three quarters of the visible map, and a handle in its corner sizes it

- Status: Accepted; supersedes [0123](0123-popup-grid-and-scroll-cap.md) in part (the 200px card floor) and [0266](0266-every-card-is-a-stack-of-foldable-layer-sections.md) in part (the 48px body floor)
- Date: 2026-11-03
- Decider: the maintainer (TJ), 2026-11-03, on PR #845 (items 7 and 9 of the review); the drag's free height and the handle over the body's corner on 2026-11-04, and the drag's least width and height later the same day
- Issues and PRs: #832, PR #845
- Browser test: `frontend/e2e/cardResize.spec.ts`
- Cited in code as: TJ, 2026-11-03
- Guide: `utils/popup/` guide: `popupFit.ts`, `popupChrome.ts`; `map/` guide: `mapPopups.ts`, `destinationCards.ts`; `src/` guide: `iconPaths.ts`; `STYLES.md`, `CARD_RESIZE`; `USAGE.md`, the card paragraphs

## Context

A card's body was capped only where the free map could not hold it, so on a tall desk nothing kept a card with every layer on from running most of the map's height. Two floors guarded the cap, each its own number: `MIN_CAPPED_PX` (200px of card) at open, below which the card was left whole and panned, and `LEAST_BODY_PX` (48px of body) for a card that grew after it opened. Neither followed the map's height. A card's size was the app's alone: a reader who wanted a wider grid or a shorter card had no way to ask for one.

## Decision

- **A ceiling.** A card's body opens no taller than three quarters of the visible map's height (`BODY_CEILING_SHARE` in `utils/popup/popupFit.ts`), even where the map has room for all of it. Past that the body scrolls under its header. The visible map is `regionNow` in `map/mapPopups.ts`: the canvas less the results sheet.
- **A floor.** A capped body keeps at least a quarter of the visible map's height (`BODY_FLOOR_SHARE`). Where the fit cannot give a body that much where its card stands, the body keeps it and the map pans, title first, as it already did for a card that does not fit. A body shorter than the floor is never capped.
- **Both bounds hold at open and on a later cap; the floor holds the reader's drag too.** They are the cap the app chooses, and the floor is also the least a drag leaves a body (below). `openingBodyCap` caps a card as it opens: the fit's cap (`capHeight`) under the ceiling, never under the floor. `grownBodyCap` caps a card that grew where it stands, and says how far the floor runs past the map's edge, which `bodyCap` pans in. A map with no visible height caps nothing, since there is nothing to fit a card to.
- **A handle in the corner.** Every card ends on a handle in its bottom-right corner (the action's foot it first stood over moved into the title row the same day, record 0266): a diagonal grip (`GRIP` in `iconPaths.ts`, at the `chip` step) in the popup's glyph grey, taking the accent under a pointer as the fold's chevron does. Its box is `CARD_RESIZE` in `styles.ts`: 16px at a desk and 32px under a finger, a phone control's size (record [0223](0223-finger-controls-draw-32.md)), pinned to the card's corner so the grip stays there as the box grows up and to the left. The body ends on the grip's own 16px at both pointer sizes (`CARD_BODY_END`, 2026-11-04): at a desk the handle stands under the last section, and under a finger its box rises 16px over the body's last corner. The last heading's chevron gives up the bottom 16px of its 32px box across its right 22px to the handle there, the handle's box running on 10px past the chevron's to the card's edge, and keeps the rest.
- **The drag.** A pointer or a finger on the handle (`setPointerCapture`) sizes the card's width from the width it opened at to the map less 20px (`cardWidthRange` in `popupChrome.ts`), and its body's height from the floor to the free map's height (2026-11-04). The opening width is the card's content box as MapLibre first drew it, which is what its own lines ask for (`holdCard` holds it, and `openCard` measures it before a reader's width is set); `POPUP_MIN_WIDTH_PX` (180px) stays only as the least for a card whose lines ask for less, which `popupCard`'s `min-width` already keeps. The body's least is the floor, a quarter of the visible map (`bodyBounds`), or the body's own height where it holds less, since under that there is nothing to scroll into (`bodyContentHeight`, read under the height the body stands at). Its most is the free map's height, the visible map less the fit's 8px at each edge, the marker's 8 and the tip's 10, less the card's header (`dragBodyRange` in `popupFit.ts`). The ceiling does not bound it: it exists to make a tall card scroll by default, not to refuse a reader who asks for more. The body stands at the height dragged to whatever it holds (`fixPopupBody`, a `min-height` beside the `max-height`), so a drag up on a ranked card scrolls its forecast under one heading, and a drag down on a short card leaves the card's surface under its last section. MapLibre stands a card centred on its point, so the width takes twice the pointer's travel and the handle stays under the pointer; the tip stays on the point because the library places a card by a share of its own width.
- **The size follows the reader.** The size a drag leaves holds for every card opened after it until the page is reloaded (`chosen` in `map/destinationCards.ts`, beside the folds), the maintainer's one rule for folds, the height as well as the width (2026-11-04). A width chosen on a narrower card does not narrow a card whose lines ask for more: it opens at the larger of the two. A card opened at that size stands its body at that height, held to the drag's range on the map it opens on, and is fitted and panned as any card is; it is never capped again, since it holds what the reader asked for. A drag on a card with no body, a spot with no layer on, sizes its width alone.
- **Pointer-only.** The handle is `aria-hidden`, has no name and is never focused. A name would be a new string for a control the keyboard cannot work, and a keyboard reader has the body's scroll for what a short card hides. Flagged to the maintainer, 2026-11-04.

## Evidence

On the branch before this change (`ec48fd6b`), `openCard` capped a body only when `capHeight` found the card too tall for the free map, and `capHeight` gave up under a 200px card. `bodyCap` kept a grown body at 48px or more. On a 1000px map a card of 800px with a 760px body stood whole and uncapped; it now caps at 750. On a 300px map a card with 200px of header and foot used to be left whole and panned; its body now keeps 75px and the card pans title first (`popupFit.test.ts`, `mapPopups.test.ts`).

On the branch at `3eb0ba1f` (2026-11-04) the maintainer found the drag would not make a card taller: `resize` held the body to `boundBody` of the floor and the ceiling, so a ranked card capped at three quarters of the map could not open past it, a body shorter than its content's cap only took `max-height`, which cannot grow a short card, and the next card's height was held to the ceiling again as a `roof`. On a 1280 by 900 map with 50px of header the drag then ran the body from 48px to 816 (`destinationCards.test.ts`), and the browser suite dragged a ranked card to one heading, a short spot card 150px past its content, and a capped card at a 600px viewport to the free map's height (`cardResize.spec.ts`); the last two still stand.

On the branch at `da5ee93f` (2026-11-04) the maintainer dragged a card down to both of the drag's least bounds, 180px of width and 48px of body (`DRAG_BODY_MIN_PX`), and got a card with its title, coordinates and section heading cut with their ellipses and a body cut mid-row. The drag now stops at the width the card opened at and at the floor: on the 1280 by 900 map of `destinationCards.test.ts` a card opened at 320px stops there and a 400px body at 225, and the stub's 150px body, under the floor, at 150. `cardResize.spec.ts` drags a ranked card to the page's top left corner and finds it at its opening width, its body at a quarter of the canvas, and no line cut that was whole before.

On a phone the body's end was 32px, the handle's box under a finger, which under a folded last section read as an empty section (the maintainer, 2026-11-04).

The grip on the card's surface, measured 2026-11-04 in `styles.test.ts`: the glyph grey reads 5.56:1 in dark and 8.34:1 in light, and the accent under a pointer 6.71:1 and 5.74:1, each over the 3:1 a graphic owes.

## Alternatives rejected

- A handle that names itself, or a keyboard path to the size: both need a new string, and a size is a convenience the body's scroll already covers for a keyboard.
- A drag held to where its card stands, as the fit is: the size a reader chose is theirs to keep.
- A drag held between the floor and the ceiling, as this record first decided (2026-11-03): the maintainer found it refused the taller card a reader asks for, and that a short card could not be made taller at all (2026-11-04).
- A drag down to 180px of width and 48px of body, as this record next decided (2026-11-04): it cut the title, the coordinates and the section heading, and left a body cut mid-row, which no reader asks for.
- A chosen width that narrows every later card: it cuts the lines of a card that asks for more.
- A body end as tall as the handle's 32px box under a finger, as this record first decided: an empty strip under a folded last section.
- A size kept for the card it was dragged on only: a second rule beside the folds', which follow the reader to every card.

## Consequences

`capHeight` takes its `min` from its caller and `MIN_CAPPED_PX` is gone, as are `LEAST_BODY_PX` and `DRAG_BODY_MIN_PX`. `cardWidthRange` takes the card's opening width, `dragBodyRange` its body's own height, and `popupChrome.ts` gains `bodyContentHeight`. `MapPopups` gains `cardBounds(popup)`, the drag's width and height ranges for that card, and `sizeCard`, and `openCard` a `size`, whose height the body stands at; `refit` takes the reader's height as `body`. A reader's height is a fixed body (`fixPopupBody` in `popupChrome.ts`) rather than a ceiling, so `openingBodyCap` and `grownBodyCap` take no `roof` and `boundBody` is gone. `popupCard` gains `position:relative` on its root for the handle, and every card's markup ends on it.
