# 0270. A card's body stands between a quarter and three quarters of the visible map, and a handle in its corner sizes it

- Status: Accepted; supersedes [0123](0123-popup-grid-and-scroll-cap.md) in part (the 200px card floor) and [0266](0266-every-card-is-a-stack-of-foldable-layer-sections.md) in part (the 48px body floor); amends [0255](0255-chart-metrics-as-panels.md) (a double press on a pinned chart card's corner)
- Date: 2026-11-03
- Decider: the maintainer (TJ), 2026-11-03, on PR #845 (items 7 and 9 of the review); the drag's free height and the handle over the body's corner on 2026-11-04, and the drag's least width and height later the same day; the double press that puts a card back, on every corner that sizes one, also on 2026-11-04
- Issues and PRs: #832, PR #845
- Browser test: `frontend/e2e/cardResize.spec.ts`
- Cited in code as: TJ, 2026-11-03
- Guide: `utils/popup/` guide: `popupFit.ts`, `popupChrome.ts`; `map/` guide: `mapPopups.ts`, `destinationCards.ts`; `utils/` guide: `doublePress.ts`; `components/` guide: `TimeSeriesChart.tsx`, `ResizeGrip.tsx`; `hooks/` guide: `useGripDrag.ts`; `src/` guide: `iconPaths.ts`; `STYLES.md`, `CARD_RESIZE`; `USAGE.md`, the card paragraphs and the chart's pinned cards

## Context

A card's body was capped only where the free map could not hold it, so on a tall desk nothing kept a card with every layer on from running most of the map's height. Two floors guarded the cap, each its own number: `MIN_CAPPED_PX` (200px of card) at open, below which the card was left whole and panned, and `LEAST_BODY_PX` (48px of body) for a card that grew after it opened. Neither followed the map's height. A card's size was the app's alone: a reader who wanted a wider grid or a shorter card had no way to ask for one.

## Decision

- **A ceiling.** A card's body opens no taller than three quarters of the visible map's height (`BODY_CEILING_SHARE` in `utils/popup/popupFit.ts`), even where the map has room for all of it. Past that the body scrolls under its header. The visible map is `regionNow` in `map/mapPopups.ts`: the canvas less the results sheet.
- **A floor.** A capped body keeps at least a quarter of the visible map's height (`BODY_FLOOR_SHARE`). Where the fit cannot give a body that much where its card stands, the body keeps it and the map pans, title first, as it already did for a card that does not fit. A body shorter than the floor is never capped.
- **Both bounds hold at open and on a later cap; the floor holds the reader's drag too.** They are the cap the app chooses, and the floor is also the least a drag leaves a body (below). `openingBodyCap` caps a card as it opens: the fit's cap (`capHeight`) under the ceiling, never under the floor. `grownBodyCap` caps a card that grew where it stands, and says how far the floor runs past the map's edge, which `bodyCap` pans in. A map with no visible height caps nothing, since there is nothing to fit a card to.
- **A handle in the corner.** Every card ends on a handle in its bottom-right corner (the action's foot it first stood over moved into the title row the same day, record 0266): a diagonal grip (`GRIP` in `iconPaths.ts`, at the `chip` step) in the popup's glyph grey, taking the accent under a pointer as the fold's chevron does. Its box is `CARD_RESIZE` in `styles.ts`: 16px at a desk and 32px under a finger, a phone control's size (record [0223](0223-finger-controls-draw-32.md)), pinned to the card's corner so the grip stays there as the box grows up and to the left. The body ends on the grip's own 16px at both pointer sizes (`CARD_BODY_END`, 2026-11-04): at a desk the handle stands under the last section, and under a finger its box rises 16px over the body's last corner. The last heading's chevron gives up the bottom 16px of its 32px box across its right 22px to the handle there, the handle's box running on 10px past the chevron's to the card's edge, and keeps the rest.
- **The drag.** A pointer or a finger on the handle (`setPointerCapture`) sizes the card's width from the width it opened at to the room where the card stands, and its body's height from the floor to that room (2026-11-04). The opening width is the card's content box as MapLibre first drew it, which is what its own lines ask for (`holdCard` holds it); `POPUP_MIN_WIDTH_PX` (180px) stays only as the least for a card whose lines ask for less, which `popupCard`'s `min-width` already keeps. The body's least is the floor, a quarter of the visible map (`bodyBounds`), or the body's own height where it holds less, since under that there is nothing to scroll into (`bodyContentHeight`, read under the height the body stands at). Its most is the room where the card stands (`standingRoom` in `popupFit.ts`): MapLibre centres a card on its point, so its width stops at twice the distance from its point to the nearer side of the visible map, less the fit's 8px at each edge, and its body at the edge on the side of its point the card stands on, less the tip, the 8px and the card's header (`dragBodyRange`). The maintainer first asked for the whole free map and then dragged a card off the map and under the panel and the legend, which no fit brings back without moving the map (2026-11-04). The ceiling does not bound it: it exists to make a tall card scroll by default, not to refuse a reader who asks for more. The body stands at the height dragged to whatever it holds (`fixPopupBody`, a `min-height` beside the `max-height`), so a drag up on a ranked card scrolls its forecast under one heading, and a drag down on a short card leaves the card's surface under its last section. MapLibre stands a card centred on its point, so the width takes twice the pointer's travel and the handle stays under the pointer; the tip stays on the point because the library places a card by a share of its own width.
- **The drag stops short of what stands over the map** (2026-11-04). The scale, the credits, the legend and the rows stand over a card, so one dragged under them had its corner, and the handle in it, out of reach. `clearSize` in `popupFit.ts` stops the side the drag pushed into each `data-map-overlay` surface the card would reach within the fit's 8px: the width where widening alone reaches it, as a legend beside the card, the height where growing taller alone does, as the scale under it, so widening a card never makes it shorter; one only the card's corner reaches gives up whichever side leaves more; never under the drag's least; what the card already stood over when the drag began is not the drag's to clear (`cardBounds`' `clear`).
- **A wheel over a card is the map's** (2026-11-04). A card rides its point, so a zoom out draws it under the cursor; the card stands outside the box MapLibre hears the wheel on, and the zoom stopped dead there. `mapPopups.ts` hands the wheel to the map (`wheelIsTheMaps`): within 400 ms of the map's last wheel (`WHEEL_GESTURE_MS`), so a zoom under way carries on across the card; for a pinch, which a browser sends as a wheel with Ctrl held; and wherever the card has nothing to scroll under the cursor. A wheel that starts on a body with more to show scrolls it. Leaflet stops the wheel on its popups on purpose and MapLibre's default does the same by construction; neither was written for a card the zoom itself moves under the cursor.
- **The size is the card's alone.** A drag sizes the card it is on and no other: the next card opens at its own size, as a pinned chart card does (record 0255). This record first kept the size for every card opened after it, the folds' rule; the maintainer reversed that on 2026-11-04, on finding the next card open at the dragged size. A body a reader sized is never capped again while its card is open, since it holds what the reader asked for. A drag on a card with no body, a spot with no layer on, sizes its width alone.
- **A card the map moved under is left alone** (2026-11-04). Once the reader zooms or pans with a card open, or the app moves the map for anything but a card's own fit or a table row's flight, the card's place is the reader's (`loose` in `map/destinationCards.ts`, told from the fit's own pans by `CARD_FIT_MOVE` on their move events). A redraw after that, the ground read at the new zoom or a section the move brought in, takes the ceiling alone (`bodyCap` with `stand` false): it is not capped against the edge it hangs past and the map is not panned for it. Before, the first redraw after a zoom capped the card to what was left of the map on its side of its point, down to the floor, and panned the map to bring the floor in, so the card shrank and jumped back into view as the zoom stopped. A double press on the handle, the reader's own ask, still fits the card again.
- **A double press puts it back** (2026-11-04). A double click, or a double tap, on the handle starts no drag and puts the card back at the size it opens at with no reader's choice: the width its own lines asked for (`cardBounds`' least) and its body under the app's cap. The card is fitted again where it stands, as a table row's card that changed size after it landed is (`refit`): capped, placed on the side the fit picks and the map panned where it must, its point kept. The same double press on a pinned chart card's corner (record 0255) puts it back at the size it was pinned at, the hover card's, and moves it back inside the plotting area where that size runs past where it stands; a pinned card's size is its own, so there is nothing to forget. Every corner that sizes a card takes it; the results area's grips and a phone sheet's already put their panel back on a double press (record [0146](0146-sheet-grips-drag.md)), and a column's edge is not a corner. The browser's `dblclick` cannot be heard: the drag starts on `pointerdown`, and a finger's second tap on a handle that captured the pointer may raise none. So each handle times its own presses (`secondPress` in `utils/doublePress.ts`): a second press within `DOUBLE_PRESS_MS` (350 ms, the grips' window, moved there from `useGripDrag`) of the last, and within `DOUBLE_PRESS_SLOP_PX` (32px, the side of `CARD_RESIZE`'s box under a finger) of it on either axis, so a quick grab of a handle a drag just moved is a drag of its own. A double tap's touch end is cancelled (`cancelTouchEnd`, moved there with it, #837), since the card shrinks out from under the finger and Firefox for Android aims the tap's click at whatever is left there.
- **Pointer-only.** The handle is `aria-hidden`, has no name and is never focused. A name would be a new string for a control the keyboard cannot work, and a keyboard reader has the body's scroll for what a short card hides. Flagged to the maintainer, 2026-11-04.

## Evidence

On the branch before this change (`ec48fd6b`), `openCard` capped a body only when `capHeight` found the card too tall for the free map, and `capHeight` gave up under a 200px card. `bodyCap` kept a grown body at 48px or more. On a 1000px map a card of 800px with a 760px body stood whole and uncapped; it now caps at 750. On a 300px map a card with 200px of header and foot used to be left whole and panned; its body now keeps 75px and the card pans title first (`popupFit.test.ts`, `mapPopups.test.ts`).

On the branch at `3eb0ba1f` (2026-11-04) the maintainer found the drag would not make a card taller: `resize` held the body to `boundBody` of the floor and the ceiling, so a ranked card capped at three quarters of the map could not open past it, a body shorter than its content's cap only took `max-height`, which cannot grow a short card, and the next card's height was held to the ceiling again as a `roof`. On a 1280 by 900 map with 50px of header the drag then ran the body from 48px to 816 (`destinationCards.test.ts`), and the browser suite dragged a ranked card to one heading, a short spot card 150px past its content, and a capped card at a 600px viewport to the free map's height (`cardResize.spec.ts`); the last two still stand.

On the branch at `da5ee93f` (2026-11-04) the maintainer dragged a card down to both of the drag's least bounds, 180px of width and 48px of body (`DRAG_BODY_MIN_PX`), and got a card with its title, coordinates and section heading cut with their ellipses and a body cut mid-row. The drag now stops at the width the card opened at and at the floor: on the 1280 by 900 map of `destinationCards.test.ts` a card opened at 320px stops there and a 400px body at 225, and the stub's 150px body, under the floor, at 150. `cardResize.spec.ts` drags a ranked card to the page's top left corner and finds it at its opening width, its body at a quarter of the canvas, and no line cut that was whole before.

On the branch at `223bd2d2` (2026-11-04) the maintainer asked for a way back to the size a card opens at, on every corner resizer. `destinationCards.test.ts` drags a spot card to 360px and a 350px body, presses it again 2 s later and again 100 ms after that 200px away, each a drag, then double presses it a few pixels from the last press: it stands at its 280px opening width with its body uncapped, `refit` called once, and a move after it sizing nothing. `TimeSeriesChart.test.tsx` drags a pinned card's corner twice and double presses it, and the card is `w-max` at its pinned height again. `cardResize.spec.ts` drags a ranked card 80px right and 120 down, double clicks the handle, and finds it at its opening width and body height, and the same row's card opens at that size again.

On the branch at `a8927287` (2026-11-04) the maintainer dragged a card past the map's right and bottom edges, under the panel and the legend, found the next card opening at that size, and found that a zoom with a card open ended with the card shrunk and the map moved. On the 1280 by 900 map of `destinationCards.test.ts` a card on the map's centre with 50px of header now stops at a 382px body where it ran to 816; `mapPopups.test.ts` stands a card at (1100, 900) on a 1280 by 1000 map and finds its width held to 344px and its body to 842 above its point; `cardResize.spec.ts` drags a ranked card 2000px down and right and finds it inside the map's edges, and opens the same row's card again at its first size. `destinationCards.test.ts` moves the map under a card and lands a section on it: `bodyCap` is asked with `stand` false and `refit` is not called. Later that day the maintainer dragged a card's corner under the scale and the credits, found a zoom out stop on the card it drew under the cursor, and found a click under a smoke plume open a new card rather than dismiss the open one (record 0253). `popupFit.test.ts` stops a 400 by 800 card 8px over credits 700px down the map, at 582px, and a 700px one 8px short of a legend beside it, at 504; `cardResize.spec.ts` drags a ranked card 2000px down and right and finds it under no `data-map-overlay` surface, and wheels over a card's header and finds the scale changed.

On a phone the body's end was 32px, the handle's box under a finger, which under a folded last section read as an empty section (the maintainer, 2026-11-04).

The grip on the card's surface, measured 2026-11-04 in `styles.test.ts`: the glyph grey reads 5.56:1 in dark and 8.34:1 in light, and the accent under a pointer 6.71:1 and 5.74:1, each over the 3:1 a graphic owes.

## Alternatives rejected

- A handle that names itself, or a keyboard path to the size: both need a new string, and a size is a convenience the body's scroll already covers for a keyboard.
- A drag free of where its card stands, up to the whole free map, as this record decided on 2026-11-04: a card dragged that far ran off the map and under the panel and the legend.
- A drag held between the floor and the ceiling, as this record first decided (2026-11-03): the maintainer found it refused the taller card a reader asks for, and that a short card could not be made taller at all (2026-11-04).
- A drag down to 180px of width and 48px of body, as this record next decided (2026-11-04): it cut the title, the coordinates and the section heading, and left a body cut mid-row, which no reader asks for.
- A body end as tall as the handle's 32px box under a finger, as this record first decided: an empty strip under a folded last section.
- A size kept for every card opened after the drag, the folds' rule, as this record decided on 2026-11-03: the maintainer found a card opening at a size dragged on another one wrong, and a pinned chart card already keeps its size to itself.
- A wheel over a card that always zooms the map: a capped card's body could then be scrolled only by its bar.
- A card that lets the pointer through while the map zooms (`pointer-events: none`): the wheel would reach the map, and so would a click meant for the card's button.
- A card fitted again after every move of the map, so it always stands whole: the map then moves under a reader who is zooming it, and the card changes size in their hands.
- The browser's `dblclick`, or a second press timed alone with no slop, on a corner: the first never arrives on a handle whose drag starts on `pointerdown`, and the second takes a quick grab of a handle a drag just moved for a double press.

## Consequences

`capHeight` takes its `min` from its caller and `MIN_CAPPED_PX` is gone, as are `LEAST_BODY_PX` and `DRAG_BODY_MIN_PX`. `cardWidthRange` takes the card's opening width, `dragBodyRange` its body's own height, and `popupChrome.ts` gains `bodyContentHeight`. `MapPopups` gains `cardBounds(popup)`, the drag's width and height ranges for that card, and `sizeCard`, and `openCard` a `size`, whose height the body stands at; `refit` takes the reader's height as `body`. A reader's height is a fixed body (`fixPopupBody` in `popupChrome.ts`) rather than a ceiling, so `openingBodyCap` and `grownBodyCap` take no `roof` and `boundBody` is gone. `popupCard` gains `position:relative` on its root for the handle, and every card's markup ends on it. `utils/doublePress.ts` holds `DOUBLE_PRESS_MS` and `cancelTouchEnd`, which `useGripDrag` imports from it rather than owning, and `secondPress`; a card in `destinationCards.ts` keeps its handle's last press (`presses`), and `restore` puts it back.
