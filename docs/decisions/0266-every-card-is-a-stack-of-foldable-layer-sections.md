## Context

Record 0253 gave every card that names a place one builder and one map module, and put the rain radar and the snow depth on the spot's card as sections. The four hazard layers were left as they were: a fire perimeter, a closure, a smoke plume and a satellite hotspot each opened a card of its own, built by its own function in its own module (`wildfirePopupHtml`, `closurePopupHtml`, `smokePopupHtml` and `hotspotPopupHtml`). A shape that opened ended the click, and the radar and the snow were asked only when no shape had, so a hotspot under a storm opened a card that said nothing about the rain. The hazards reached a destination's card only as two amber lines between its band and its grid, the nearest fire and the closure it stood in, read from the table's warning maps rather than from the layers on the map. A pending dot's card and a label's carried neither.

The maintainer asked on 2026-11-03 for one common object: every layer merging into one card, the same sections on the pending and ranked cards as on the spot's, and a chevron on each section that folds it.

## Decision

- **Every card on the map is one stack of sections under its band**: a ranked marker's, a pending dot's, a basemap label's and the spot's. There is one section for each layer that is on in the Layers menu and has something at the card's point, in the map's click rank: `Fire activity`, `Trail closures`, `Active wildfires`, `Area closures`, `Smoke`, `Rain radar`, `Snow depth` (`POINT_TARGETS` in `utils/mapClick.ts`). A hazard's shape opens the spot's card at the click, a star's at the star's own point, and that card carries the hazard's section beside every other layer's, so a hotspot over snow in a closed area under rain is one card of four sections.
- **Each section is one shape** (`cardSection` in `utils/popup/popupChrome.ts`): a heading, `{layer}: {name}`, the layer being the Layers menu's own row, in the singular where the row is plural (`Active wildfire: Bolt Creek`, `Area closure: Probe Fire Closure`, `Trail closure: …`, `Fire activity: Hotspot`, `Smoke: Heavy smoke`), since a section names the one thing at the point where the menu and the legend name the layer; the link-out glyph beside the name where the thing has a page, NIFC's map for a fire and the order's page for a closure, named `View on NIFC map` and `View closure order` for a screen reader; a closure order's type in amber under the name (`Safety Closure`); the rows; and the fine print `{source}: {when}`, `NIFC:`, `NASA FIRMS:`, `NOAA HMS:`, `US Forest Service:`, `NEXRAD radar:` or `NOAA snow analysis:`, which replaces the old cards' `Updated:`, `Effective:` and `Observed:`. A closure's span still never says it is active, for 0161's reason.
- **The reach is the table's where the table has one, and the point's where it does not** (`utils/hazardSections.ts`): a fire or a hotspot within 10 miles of the point (`FIRE_WARN_MILES`, record 0020); an area closure only where the point stands inside its outer ring, as the Closure column tests; a closed trail, road or site within a mile (`CLOSED_TRAIL_MILES`); a plume only over the point, the densest where HMS nests them. A section about a thing the point is not on starts with a `Distance` row, how far and which of eight compass points, `3.2 mi NW`.
- **The heading stands on the band** (the maintainer chose mockup 1 of ten in the design lab, 2026-11-03): the header band's fill, `--popup-band`, run from the card's edge to its edge with the rows' 10px inset inside it, at least 24px tall; the layer's word and its colon at 600 and the name at 400, both in the ink, on one line cut with an ellipsis; the link-out glyph and the chevron at the right. The fills part the sections, with 8px of the card's surface between one and the next and 4px between a heading and its body, and the hairline that ruled each later section off is gone. The section headings are the only full-width band below the header, the grid's columns being the same fill in narrow strips; the forecast's heading wears it too.
- **A row's key leads**: the key at 600 in the ink, the value at 400 in the label grey in the values' mono face (`row`). The grey key over a dark value it replaced read as inverted. The header's facts line keeps its look. The forecast grid takes the rows' split (2026-11-04): every head at 600 in the ink, every value at 400 in the label grey's mono.
- **A fire, a hotspot and a closure are warnings** (`PointSection.warning`, set by `wildfireSectionAt`, `hotspotSectionAt` and `closureSectionAt`); smoke, the radar, the snow and the forecast are not, being weather. A warning's heading lays the table's amber over the band (`--popup-warn-band`: 15% in dark, 30% in light) and stands the ⚠️ the results table's Wildfire and Closure columns open on before the layer's word, in a 1.5em slot of its own so a cut name never moves it. The mark is text, as the table's is, so a screen reader names it and the app adds no word. A closure order's amber type line stays as it was.
- **The folds**: before an analysis, on a pending, label or spot card, every section stands open; on a ranked card the forecast and every warning stand open and the rest folded to their names, since the grid is what the marker was clicked for and a reason to stay away is worth reading before it. A fold the reader makes on any card holds for that section on every card until the page is reloaded (`map/destinationCards.ts` keeps the choices), so a reader who shut the smoke on one marker is not shown it open on the next.
- **The forecast is a ranked card's last section**: `Forecast: {model}`, the model the numbers came from (`Forecast: NOAA GFS`), or `Forecast` alone over an archive report, which no model produced; the grid; and the fine print `Analyzed: Nov 3, 2026, 1:36 PM`, when the report committed (`analyzedAtMs` from `hooks/useAnalysisReport.ts`). Last, because the hazards are what a reader weighs the forecast against.
- **The sections follow the layers.** A layer switched off takes its section off every open card and leaves the card; a fetch that lands after a pan adds its sections to the open cards it reaches; the radar's section follows the player as before (record 0166). A layer that is off adds nothing, so the ranked card's two amber warning lines are gone and the table's Wildfire and Closure columns keep their always-on checks.
- **A hotspot's fine print is when the app last fetched the detections**, the response's `fetched_at`, because the detection's own time is already its `Detected` row.
- **The fold button is named for the layer** (`aria-label`, the heading's word for the layer) and says whether it is open through `aria-expanded`, so a screen reader hears "Active wildfire, expanded" rather than a `Hide` or `Show` of the app's own. It is a chevron from `iconPaths.ts`, down while open and up while folded as the results bar's is, in a new role, `CARD_FOLD` in `styles.ts`: a 24px box, 32px under a finger, in the popup's glyph grey, taking the accent under a pointer as the band's link-out glyph does.

## Evidence

On the branch as it stood on 2026-11-03 before this change (`dabb0fe7`), `mapPopups.ts` asked the point targets only after every shape under the click had passed, so a click that a hotspot, a fire, a closure or a plume answered never reached the radar or the snow; the four hazard cards were four builders in four modules, each with its own facts line (`Wildfire | 138,285 acres`, `Area closure | 4,210 acres`) and its own date label; and the ranked card's two warning lines came from `firePopupText` and `closurePopupText` over `MapInputs.fireWarnings` and `closureWarnings`, the table's maps, which a pending, label or spot card never read.

The change that followed, `d5afce7f` on 2026-11-03, touched 36 files, 1,048 lines in and 656 out: `hazardSections.ts` is new, the four builders and the two popup texts are gone, and every overlay registers a point target that reads from the features it already holds, so a card asks no upstream for its sections.

The light band (measured 2026-11-03 in `styles.test.ts`): the dark band, halfway from the panel to the raised step, reads 1.18:1 on the dark panel, and the same half step in light read 1.06:1 on the light panel and all but vanished. `--ui-band` is also the chart's heads and the panel title chip, so it stays, and the light popup's `--popup-band` is `color-mix(in oklab, var(--ui-raised-hover) 50%, var(--ui-raised))`, halfway between the raised step and its hover, which reads 1.18:1 on the light panel. Its ranked cell moves from `--ui-raised` to `--ui-raised-hover`, so it stays a step darker than its column (1.06:1, as before). On the new light band the ink reads 12.88:1, the label grey 7.05:1, the glyph's hover 4.85:1 and the scroll thumb 3.36:1, and the ranked family's light inks over their tints 4.69:1 at worst, where the panel's worst was 5.42:1.

The warning band, measured the same day: the heading's ink 7.05:1 dark and 8.2:1 light, the grey glyphs 3.3:1 and 4.48:1, the accent under the pointer 3.99:1 and 3.09:1. At 20% in dark the grey glyphs read 2.94:1, under the 3:1 a graphic owes, which is what holds the dark amber at 15%.

A distance under a twentieth of a mile prints no `Distance` row (`ON_IT_MILES`), so a card opened on a star or inside a perimeter says nothing about how far the thing is. A card that grows past the free map after it opened, a section having landed on it, caps its body where it stands and keeps at least 48 px of it (`LEAST_BODY_PX` in `mapPopups.ts`, 2026-11-03).

## Alternatives rejected

- A closure and a closed trail reaching 10 miles, as a fire does: the maintainer chose inside-only for an area, as the Closure column tests, and a mile for a trail, road or site, because a closed trail says nothing about the summit it leads to and a reader standing on a peak beside it is not on it (2026-11-03).
- Sections drawn per card kind, the spot's, the label's and the ranked card's each with its own, and no shared folds: the maintainer asked for one object, and a fold that held on one card and not the next would be a second rule to learn.
- Keeping the amber warning band on the ranked card beside the sections: a fire within reach would be said twice, once in amber and once as a section, which is 0161's reason a fire's card never carried a notice.
- A `Hide` and `Show` word on the fold: a new string, where the layer's name and `aria-expanded` already say both.
- The nine other heading looks in the design lab (2026-11-03): the layer word grey and the name bold; the legend's swatch in place of the word; the word in a chip; the word and the name on two lines; a rule over the heading and no fill, which all but vanished in light; the raised step as the fill, where a warning's amber pushed the grey glyphs under 3:1 and they had to take the ink; the name as a first row; a two-cell heading over right-aligned rows; and a rail down each section's left edge, faint in dark.
- A plural heading, the Layers row word for word (`Active wildfires: Bolt Creek`, as this record first shipped): it named one fire with the layer's plural.
- A second PR for the sections: folded into #845, the PR that made the spot card the sections' home (the maintainer, 2026-11-03).

## Consequences

`PointSection` carries `kind`, the target it is a reading of, which is the key its fold is kept under and what takes it off a card when its layer goes off, and `link`, `warn` and `warning` for the hazards: `warn` the closure order's amber line, `warning` whether the section is one. `sectionOpen` takes the flag, and `map/destinationCards.ts` hands it the section's own when a chevron is pressed, so the first press folds a warning a ranked card drew open. `MapPoint` moved to `utils/pointReadout.ts`, since the hazard readers in utils take one.

`mapPopups.ts` no longer opens the spot's card with sections in hand: `openSpot` opens it at a point, `pointKinds` and `readPoint` are what every card asks its layers, `onPointChange` is how a card hears that one changed, and `bodyCap` caps a card that grew. The spot card has no owner on the board, since what it carries is the layers' sections.

The sections are in a card's first paint. Asked after the card opened, each landed a frame later, so the fit placed a card that then grew: a table row's card, whose sections landed during its flight, was capped against its point mid-air to the body's 48 px floor, or grew past the map's top once the flight landed, and a second click on the row showed a different card (the maintainer on the preview at dcc68a5, 2026-11-03). A point target's `read` now answers at once wherever it holds what it needs (`PointAnswer`): the hazards always, and the radar and the snow once their tile is decoded, which the tile under a pointer is by the time it clicks (`heldPixel`). `destinationCards.ts` asks every layer before it draws the card, so `openCard` measures the whole card and the flight folds in the fit of it, and a card that still grows while the map moves is capped where the move sets it down. A layer's `refresh` answers only a card that carries its section, so the radar coming on over a dry point no longer adds the no-echo line a card opened after it would not show. The snow reads its open cards again as its tiles arrive, as the radar does, and keeps each point's reading while it is on, since a card open when it came on had nothing to read until a tile arrived and so never took its section.

A table row's card for a destination outside the view cannot carry its hazards or its tiles in its first paint: the overlays fetch for the view on `moveend`, so those sections join the card after its flight lands, on a card the fit placed without them. Such a card fits again once (the maintainer, 2026-11-04), at the first section that changes its size after it lands. `mapPopups.ts`'s `refit` measures the body uncapped, caps it between the floor and the ceiling ([0270](0270-a-card-body-between-a-quarter-and-three-quarters-and-a-resize-handle.md)), turns the card to the side the fit picks and pans the map by the fit's move, as `openCard` does, so a card that grew stands whole at its width rather than hanging off an edge or keeping a cap measured on the shorter card. One re-fit, not one per section; the map's next move, or a drag on the card's handle, ends the wait, and a fold or a press on the card's button moves nothing.

A card that changes size in the air is placed again before it lands (the maintainer, 2026-11-04, on the preview at 984f70e1: the card landed with its header under the map's top edge, and the map panned about 48px a moment later). Traced the same day on this branch's image at 1440x900 with the preview banner, every flight's first fit was right for the card it measured: the banner moves the canvas down the page, and the fit, the obstacles and the flight's offset are all in the canvas's own pixels. What moved the card was later. A picture whose tile is held but not yet decoded answers a few milliseconds after the fit (the radar and the snow joined 3 and 9ms after it, growing a 346px card to 410px in the air, upward on a card standing above its point), and the ground's slope joins every table row's card in the air, rewriting a line at the same height. The re-fit at landing fired for both. So the card keeps the box its fit measured: a redraw that leaves the box as it was moves nothing, a change in the air calls the `steer` its opener handed it, which aims the flight again (`map/centring.ts`) with the card fitted for where the flight now lands (`place`), and only a change after landing, or one in the air with no flight left to steer, re-fits. A flight aimed again, by a resize too, fits the card standing rather than opening a new one, so no layer is asked again, and a pending dot's flight does the same where it used to fly without its card's move. The landing is heard from the flight's own `moveend`, armed at its `movestart`, since a flight aimed again stops the one in the air with a `moveend` of its own (MapLibre 6.15's `flyTo` calls `stop` before it starts).

The same trace found the re-fit moving a card that had not changed at all: on a 1080x833 map a 198x346 card stood whole above its point with no move, and whole below it 42px up, clear of the forecast player. The open placed it above. At landing MapLibre projected the point as (540.0000000000239, 416.5000000000477), and `placePopup`'s exact comparison of the two areas let the noise pick the side 42px away, so the card turned under its point and the map panned after the flight had landed. Areas within half a square pixel now tie, and the shorter move wins (`AREA_SLOP` in `utils/popup/popupFit.ts`).

On a phone a tap on the map that opens a card lowers the results sheet to its bar first, as a table row's press does (record [0143](0143-row-opens-its-popup.md)), and the card opens once the sheet is down, fitted to the map it leaves (the maintainer, 2026-11-04): a marker's, a pending dot's, a label's, a hazard's and the spot's on bare map, which is as tall. The click goes through `lowerSheetFirst` (`hooks/useRowFocus.ts`), which `MapView` hands the map's controller and which opens the card at once at a desk or with the sheet already down. A pending row's press in the table lowers the sheet the same way, where it used to open its dot's card under the sheet. A tap that only closes a card, and a click while a ring is drawn, lower nothing.

`popupCard` lost its `warnings` and the notices band with its one ⚠️; `sectionHeading` became `cardSection`, which takes the layer and the name apart (`layer`, `name`) so it can weigh them apart, and `bodySection` became `cardSections`, a gap where it drew a rule. `SECTION_EDGE` rules off the action's foot alone. The ⚠️ is `WARNING_MARK` in `utils/fireProximity.ts`, which the Wildfire and Closure cells and the warning heading read. The fine print is the label's ink (`--popup-fine` in `map.css`): the faint ink it wore measured 3.07:1 on the dark card, which the browser suite's axe never read while only an overlay's card ended on one, and now holds every card to 4.5:1 (measured 2026-11-03: 5.56:1 and 8.34:1 on the card, 4.71:1 and 7.89:1 on the band, dark and light). A comparison row's model leaves the band while its forecast section names it, so a card says the model once. `MapStage` lost its `fire` and `closure` props, `MapView` its warning maps, and `controller.ts` its `fireWarningAt` and `closureWarningAt`, all replaced by `analyzedAtMs`.

The Layers menu's row names are spelled once each beside their layer (`WILDFIRE_ROW_LABEL`, `FIRE_ACTIVITY_ROW_LABEL`, `SMOKE_ROW_LABEL`, `CLOSURE_ROW_LABELS`), read by the menu and the legend, and the sections read them too, but for a fire and a closure, whose singular is spelled beside the row's (`WILDFIRE_SECTION_LABEL`, `CLOSURE_SECTION_LABELS`), so a section's heading cannot drift from the switch it answers to. The fold button takes the heading's word, so a screen reader hears `Active wildfire`.

The browser suite expects the spot's card from a click on a star, a fire or a closure, with the layer's section in it and the mark before a warning's heading, a marker's card with its fire open on a band apart from the forecast's (`fireActivity.spec.ts`, `mapPopups.spec.ts`), and reads the band's link-out glyph and the forecast's fold apart, the fold's heading on the header's band (`linkLook.spec.ts`).

The chart's hover card and panel title chip keep `--ui-band`, so in light they stand on the paler band the card left.

The forecast grid took the rows' split on 2026-11-04 (the maintainer): every head at 600 in the ink and every value at 400 in the label grey's mono, 4.71:1 and 7.05:1 on the band, but for the ranked family's tinted cells and a ranked number on `GRID_RANKED_COLOR`, which keeps the ink because the grey reads 3.94:1 there in dark. The bold heads cost 6.49px on the widest label, `Precipitation (in/hr)` at 115.52px against 109.03px at 400 (Chrome on macOS, 2026-11-04), so the marker card's cap is 381px, from 374px (the widest grid 359.4px, `WIDEST_GRID_PX` 360), and a compact band's inset 1px, from 2px, which holds a two-digit total at 318.2px in a 360px phone's 320px of body; a map takes the compact grid under 400px, from 393px.
