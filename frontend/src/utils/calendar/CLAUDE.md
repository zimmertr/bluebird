# Frontend layout: `src/utils/calendar/`

- `src/utils/calendar/calendar.ts` — the forecast window as a calendar (#166), as a barrel over the five sibling modules below, so every importer reads one name. Together they own the servable band, the `ForecastSelection` type (`now` | `days` with optional narrowed `hours`, plus the dateless Dates arm — today outlined, nothing selected, Analyze blocked — that a fresh switch to Dates opens on), month-grid generation, the click/drag reducers, and the conversion to the `datetime-local` pair everything downstream reads. All of it pure, so the node project checks every case without a page and `ForecastCalendar.test.tsx` has only the wiring left to show: the component is wiring only. **The band's far edge is the nearer of two limits, and only one of them is per model.** The hard one is the API's: `FUTURE_LIMIT_DAYS` is 15 (Open-Meteo's "16 days" counts today) and `bandEnd` walks back a further day wherever a local 23:59 lands on the next UTC date, because the request carries UTC dates. The soft one is the selected model's, carried in **hours** rather than days and passed in from `/api/capabilities`. Hours because a model's reach is set by when its last run started, so it lands mid-afternoon as readily as at midnight — rounded down to whole covered days, HRRR's 42 h would offer *today only* anywhere west of Greenwich, which for a short-range mountain model is the same as not offering it. A day the reach lands partway into draws `partial`, the state `DayAvailability` already uses for "we can serve some of this day". `clampSelection` is what a model change does to a window that no longer fits: both ends clamp independently, and it returns `null` for "unchanged" so the panel warns only when something actually moved. `classifyWindow`/`classifyAqiCoverage` read the same band so the grid and the warnings cannot disagree; they sit beside it in `calendarBand.ts`, and `urlState.ts` re-exports `classifyAqiCoverage` for the panel. Both edges now ride in one `BandLimits` object rather than as two number parameters, because 384 hours and 365 days are adjacent arguments of the same type and a transposed pair would compile. The NEAR edge is no longer compiled here at all: it is the archive's reach from `/api/capabilities` (#123), and the days it opens up draw as `full` because the archive holds every hour of them and air quality reaches over them too. It also owns how a window says its own dates: `windowPhrase`/`windowCaption` behind the results header, `archiveSeamPhrase` behind the panel's seam line, and `needsYear` — the one predicate deciding whether a date carries its year (the window's years against the reader's, either end, so both ends carry it or neither does), because a year-old report otherwise reads as four days of `Sat, Sep 13`. Every one of them takes an injectable `now`, so nothing here passes or fails on the year the suite runs in
- `src/utils/calendar/calendarDates.ts` — `YYYY-MM-DD` days and `HH:MM` times: parsing, validation (`isValidDatetimeLocal` included, which the window classifiers in `calendarBand.ts` use), and day and month arithmetic through the Date constructor's field overflow, because a local day is 23 or 25 hours on a DST transition. The rest of the calendar reads these rather than doing arithmetic of its own. `utcDayKey` is `dayKey`'s UTC twin, the day an Open-Meteo request names
- `src/utils/calendar/calendarBand.ts` — the servable band: `FUTURE_LIMIT_DAYS`, `BandLimits`, `bandStart`/`bandEnd`, `inBand`, the air-quality horizon, and the two classifiers `classifyWindow`/`classifyAqiCoverage` (`urlState.ts` re-exports `classifyAqiCoverage` for the panel, and its suite still tests both). One module, so the grid's unpickable days and the warnings' out-of-range windows are computed by the same functions
- `src/utils/calendar/calendarGrid.ts` — the month grid: `monthGrid`'s cells marked `full`/`partial`/`unservable` against the band, `monthHasBandDay` for the month navigation, and the weekday and month labels. `canPageTo` is what the arrows read (#803): into any month with a servable day, and from a month with none one step toward the band, from either side, so a calendar a link opened outside the band always has a way back. Record: [0236](../../../../docs/decisions/0236-a-share-link-restores-what-it-can.md)
- `src/utils/calendar/calendarSelection.ts` — `ForecastSelection` and its reducers: a click with a pending anchor, a drag, a clamp to a band that moved under the selection, a switch of arm, and the `datetime-local` pair a selection asks about. Reducers rather than handlers in `ForecastCalendar.tsx`, so the node project pins what a click means
- `src/utils/calendar/calendarPhrases.ts` — how a window says its own dates: `windowPhrase`/`windowCaption`, `archiveSeamPhrase`, and `needsYear`, the one rule for when a date carries its year `windowCaption` prints a Current report's `as of` off the commit time it is handed (#696), to the minute.
