# Frontend layout: `src/utils/score/`

The score family (record [0307](../../../../docs/architectural-decision-records/0307-a-score-with-lenses-ranks-the-field.md)): a destination's window scored from its hourly series on one of five lenses, computed in the browser over rows already held, so a lens change is a presentation knob and spends nothing. Every module is pure and DOM-free for the node-env Vitest. The formula is the one the maintainer approved in the PR that introduced it; a change to a curve, a weight or a rule is a change to that table and to record 0307.

- `src/utils/score/daylight.ts`: sunrise and sunset at a coordinate from NOAA's solar position equations (`sunTimes`), the destination's own solar day (`solarDay`), and whether an hour is lit for at least half of it (`isDaylightHour`). Separate because it is astronomy rather than weather, and the window score is the only reader
- `src/utils/score/factors.ts`: the seven hour factors, each 0 to 1 or null: the piecewise-linear curves (`ramp`, `WIND_CURVE`, `COMFORT_CURVE`, `WARMTH_CURVE`, `DRY_CURVE`, `AIR_CURVE`), the sky from the summit against the cloud deck's base and top (`skyFactor`, which is why the series carries `cloud_top_ft`, record 0306), and fresh snow from the 24 hours before (`freshFactor`)
- `src/utils/score/lenses.ts`: the five lenses (`LENSES`, `DEFAULT_LENS`, `isLens`) and their weights (`LENS_WEIGHTS`); a lens names no string a reader sees, the components do
- `src/utils/score/hourScore.ts`: one hour's score, the weighted geometric mean with each factor floored at `FACTOR_FLOOR`, a null factor dropped and the rest renormalized
- `src/utils/score/windowScore.ts`: a row's score over the window (`scoreRow`): every hour scored, the dark hours left out, the best run of `BEST_RUN_HOURS` consecutive lit hours in one solar day, and 0.75 of that plus 0.25 of the daylight mean. A night-only window is scored on every hour so it still ranks
- `src/utils/score/headline.ts`: the results column's count (`headline`: places at `HEADLINE_BAR` or the best ten reached, out of `total`, worded by `headlineText`), the subtitle's best hours (`bestHoursText` over the top `BEST_HOURS_ROWS`, with `hourShort`, record 0319) and the range the score's colour is stretched over (`scoreRange`); the ramp itself is `SCORE_RAMP` in `utils/colors.ts`, the colour owner
- `src/utils/score/hourStrip.ts`: the hours a row's strip draws beside its score (`stripHours`): the lit hours in order, a gap after a night, the best stretch marked. Separate from `windowScore.ts`, which decides the score, because this only lays out what the score already read
