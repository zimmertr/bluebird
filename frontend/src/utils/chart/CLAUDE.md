# Frontend layout: `src/utils/chart/`

- `src/utils/chart/chartData.ts` — the pure half of `components/TimeSeriesChart.tsx`: which families can be charted (`ChartMetric`, every family, snowfall's hourly series included since #678; `CHART_METRICS` lists them in the panel's order, each labelled in an hour's unit, `HOURLY_UNIT`, because the chart plots one hour at a time. Record: [0222](../../../../docs/decisions/0222-one-card-one-close-one-type-ramp.md)), the series field each reads, `metricForSort`, the line identity `chartKey` (unrounded, unlike `geoKey`, for the reason `points.ts` gives), aligning a row's series onto the chart's time grid, the line names (`comparedLineLabel`/`modelNamed`/`modelSuffix`, all through `lineSuffix`, and `lineNamed` for a plain line; `sharedNames` and `collidingType` add the Type column word, `The Tooth (Crag)` or `1. The Tooth (Crag, NOAA GFS)`, while two places on screen share a name, #715, and the Removed list composes through the same two), `valueAt` (which the map's playback colours read too, so a marker and the chart agree at one playhead), the axis labels, the y domain, the pixel-to-data inversions behind the hover box, the hover box's row budget, and the chart's extreme line (#699): `extremeValue`, the most extreme hourly value among the `ranked` lines, null when nothing has one or when the extreme is 0 on the `ZERO_IS_NONE` families (precipitation, snowfall, wind, AQI) where 0 is an absence rather than a reading; `extremeLabel`, its label, the hover box's number and the metric's unit (`75.4 mph`); and `extremeLabelPosition`, above the line or below it when the line runs through the top headroom. `ChartLine.ranked` is what tells the ranked lines from a comparison's: the chart sets it on every plain row, and `compareSeries` on the ranking model's pairs alone. Separate because Vitest runs with no DOM here, so nothing left in the component is tested `formatTooltipValue` is the tooltip's value: the axis's format, except a cloud deck at either edge of its walk, which prints as its bound. The chart's slots are `ChartMetrics`, one to four families (`MAX_CHART_PANELS`), opened by `metricsFor` and moved by `pickMetric` (which swaps rather than draw one family twice), `addMetric` (the first family not drawn), `removeMetric` (never the first) and `moveMetric` (a slot after the first to another, the rest closing up); `cardCells` writes a card row's bare cells (#499). `tracksCursor` is handed every path a hover redraws, so each drawn panel spends the budget again. Record: [0255](../../../../docs/decisions/0255-chart-metrics-as-panels.md)
- `src/utils/chart/chartPointer.ts` — where a press on the results chart lands in its data (#336): the plot band's geometry (`PLOT_MARGIN`, the two axis strips, `plotBand`), the hour under a pixel (`hourIndexAt`, which the click that moves the playhead takes too) and a finger's reading, the hour and the y value under it (`fingerReading`). Separate from `chartData.ts` because it is the geometry of the drawn chart rather than of its data, and the component and the tests read one copy. Record: [0144](../../../../docs/decisions/0144-chart-reads-a-finger.md) A phone panel is inset under its own picker (`PHONE_PLOT_MARGIN`, from `PHONE_PICKER_PX` and `PHONE_PICKER_INSET_PX`, #499), and `plotBand` and `fingerReading` take the margins the chart drew with. A stacked chart's top panel draws no x axis strip (`xAxisPx`), so `plotBand` and `fingerReading` take whether the panel draws one; `panelFloorPx`, `panelWidthFloorPx`, `MIN_PANEL_BAND_PX` and `MIN_PANEL_BAND_WIDTH_PX` (two of the widest ticks, `WIDEST_X_TICK_PX`) are the floors below which a panel is held or not drawn, `cardPlacement` keeps the hover card over several panels inside their area, `evenStack` and `stackAfterDrag` are a stack's shares, even and after a grip between two of its panels moves, and `gridAfterDrag` where a grid's two splits land after a gutter or the crossing moves (#499). Record: [0255](../../../../docs/decisions/0255-chart-metrics-as-panels.md)
- `src/utils/chart/chartLayout.ts`: how a desk arranges the chart's panels (#499): the layouts each count offers and opens in, their names and the button's, each as rows of panels (`rowsOf`), and a rows layout's arithmetic (`rowRects` for where each panel stands, `sharesAfterDrag` for one bar's move) that the component's drag paints from. Pure, so the link's `layout=` and the component read one definition.
- `src/utils/chart/chartCanvas.ts` — how the chart's series lines are drawn on a canvas (#499): `lineStrokes`, each line's width, opacity and point-sample dot in the `<Line>` props' spelling (`LINE_DRAW`, `CROWDED_PAST`, the focused line wider and the rest dimmed); `canvasColor`, the theme's half of a `light-dark()` pair; `drawLines`, every path broken at a missing hour; and `drawActiveDots`, the hover dots ringed in the panel colour. Pure over a context, so the node suite tests it with a recording one. Record: [0256](../../../docs/decisions/0256-chart-lines-on-canvas.md)
- `src/utils/chart/chartColors.ts` — the categorical line colours: twenty a theme, repeated past the twentieth, and `allocateColors`, the app's ONE colour allocator, which hands a colour to a charted destination and to a (destination, compared model) pair from one session-long counter, so a compared line and the destination beside it can never share a hue, and a colour once given is kept. Apart from `colors.ts` because these hues name a line rather than encode a quantity. Since #365 it also owns the chart's chrome (`CHART_CHROME`, one axis, grid, surface, playhead and unassigned colour per theme since #706), because Recharts takes a `stroke` as a prop the stylesheet cannot reach. The chart draws `CHROME`, the same pairs written as one `light-dark()` each (`themed`, #786). The chart's extreme line (#699) adds no line colour: it wears the axis colour, as the Now seam and a model's end do. The surface colour, the results panel's slate-800 in dark, is the halo every chrome label is stroked in so it reads over a series line crossing it, and `chartColors.test.ts` holds the axis colour to 4.5:1 on it (5.56:1 on the v4 hexes, measured 2026-10-13; 8.34:1 in light, 2026-10-15). Record: [0126](../../../../docs/decisions/0126-chart-marks-the-extreme-hour.md) The chrome's four slates are the hexes Tailwind v4 computes from its oklch steps rather than v3's, which were a shade off every class the panel around the chart paints, and `CHART_TICK_PX` is `MICRO_SIZE`'s 11px as the number Recharts takes, for the ticks and every chrome label (#687). Record: [0131](../../../../docs/decisions/0131-type-scale-floor-and-elevation-scale.md) The palette is ColorBrewer Set1 in twenty shades (#801), the set the maintainer chose in the Chart Palette Lab: Brewer's nine, a lighter shade of each and a darker shade of the first two, every one turned five degrees toward red. Dark is the lab's output. Light makes the darker shades first and lowers the eight under 3:1 on the panel in OKLCH lightness alone until they clear it, so nothing in a line's colour wears a rim. `colorForIndex` repeats the twenty past the twentieth, and `LINE_DRAW` with `CROWDED_PAST` is how `TimeSeriesChart` draws a line: 1.5px up to thirty lines, 1px past thirty, full strength at any count, 2.5px focused with the rest at half. `chartColors.test.ts` spells the forty hexes and pins both themes' ratios (light 3.01 to 7.21, none under 3:1; dark 2.13 to 13.48, four under, accepted with Tableau's lead, 2026-10-27). Record: [0228](../../../../docs/decisions/0228-chart-lines-set1-twenty.md) The allocator stores a slot index rather than a colour, so a theme switch recolours every line, swatch and table checkbox without moving which destination holds which slot; `colorForIndex(index, theme)` reads a slot in one theme, for the contrast tests, and `lineColor` and `paletteColors` read it in both at once, which is what the app draws. Record: [0158](../../../../docs/decisions/0158-light-theme-behind-semantic-tokens.md) Record: [0211](../../../../docs/decisions/0211-theme-switch-renders-nothing.md)
