# Frontend layout: `src/components/`

- `src/components/SkipLink.tsx` — the page's first Tab stop (#576): `Skip to map`, hidden until it has the keyboard (`SKIP_LINK`), moving the focus to the `<main>` landmark `App.tsx` renders the map and the results in. A real link to the landmark's id, but the press focuses the target itself rather than following the fragment, so no `#` rides into a link the reader copies. Its own module so a test can drive it; `App.tsx` cannot be rendered under Vitest, so `SkipLink.test.tsx` reads its source to hold the link before every other control.
- `src/components/ForecastCalendar.tsx` — the whole forecast window in one card: a **When** segment picking Current or Dates, an **Hours** segment under it, and the month grid below both. The two segments sit above the grid rather than around it, so the two decisions the window needs read as one block and neither lands below the fold; the grid and the Hours row appear only under Dates, the way the hour fields appear only under Hourly, because Current takes no input at all. "Dates" names the second arm rather than Days or Range because it must be silent about count *and* tense: 55 of the 71 days it reaches are behind today (#234). Holds no date logic of its own (see `utils/calendar.ts`); a mouse drags a range, a finger taps twice, and the selection commits on pointerup so a drag never re-renders the panel per pointermove. Cell brightness is one channel carrying one meaning — **how much of that day the app can serve** (`DayAvailability`) — which is why an adjacent month's days are drawn blank rather than dimmed. **Since #687 the Hours row and the grid open from a field** rather than standing in the panel body: under Dates the panel shows one full-width `SELECT`-styled field holding the window in the results bar's own words (`windowCaption`, so no new string), named by the When label and those words and allowed to wrap to a second line (`CALENDAR_FIELD`) because the longest window cannot fit it, and a press opens the card on `Menu.tsx`, 4px under the field at the field's width like every menu (`widthRef` and `anchorRef` both the slot under When; record [0201](../../../docs/decisions/0201-one-menu-for-every-trigger.md)), the field wearing the field shape's stroked chevron on its first line (`CALENDAR_FIELD.arrow`), with the calendar on `CALENDAR_GROUND`, the slate-900 the well had, because `DAY.range` reads by hue alone on slate-800. The field is drawn only when it has something to show: with no range there is none, choosing Dates opens the card where it would stand, a close without a pick puts the arm back on Current, and a close over a range keeps Dates and the field. A `Done` footer under the grid closes the card the same way, so closing is a control and not only a press outside. Current takes the card, the field and the Hours row away in one render, because the segment is exempt from the card's press-outside dismissal (`ownPressRef`). A card opened from the field keeps the field over it, lit, and a second press on the field closes it; a card opened from Dates with no range keeps the slot empty until it closes (`fromField`), so a first pick does not push a field in over the card's top. Current closes it. The card's state (the page, the anchor, the range to bring back) stays this component's, so a close is not a reset; Escape closes it and abandons a half-made range, and the keyboard opens on the focused day (`initialFocusRef`). Day rows are 28px on a fine pointer and 44 under touch, with the weekday initials kept at the 36px the rows had (`DAY.head`). Record: [0129](../../../docs/decisions/0129-calendar-as-a-field-and-popover.md) On a phone (`sheet`, from `ForecastSection`'s `phone`) the card is a bottom sheet headed When, through `Menu`'s `sheet`, with the sheet's Done in place of its own and every close still `dismiss`; the hook gets a closed menu there, and the slot under When is drawn for the field alone.
