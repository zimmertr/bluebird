# Frontend layout: `src/map/`

- `src/map/basemap.ts` — the map's module-level helpers, cut out of `MapView.tsx` unchanged (#410): `STYLE`, the OpenFreeMap style URL `coldLoad.test.ts` checks the preconnect hints against; `enhanceBasemap`, which patches the loaded style with the trails (`TRAIL_LAYER`, sienna from z11) and their light casing directly beneath them (`TRAIL_CASING_LAYER`, Liberty's background a pixel wider each side, added hidden and shown by `overlays/imagery.ts` alone, because the sienna reads 1.69:1 on a forest and the casing lifts it to 3.89:1 on a ground that reads 8.69:1 there; record [0234](../../../docs/decisions/0234-map-reads-on-a-dark-ground.md)) and the clickable peak and lake labels, each label with its hidden glow directly beneath it, all under the style's first label, and puts a glow directly beneath each of the style's own place labels it carries, filtered to the settlement classes a click takes, with no label of its own (#710), and from `POI_MINZOOM` gives those labels the sprite's `town_hall_11` disc with the name under it, anchored like a peak in the style's own type (`townMarker`, hamlets alone on `label_other`; each property is one zoom step written whole, since the style's own icon is a zoom step MapLibre will not nest). Record: [0157](../../../docs/decisions/0157-places-and-climbing-labels-are-selectable.md) It takes the lake class off the style's water labels, so nothing is labelled twice, and the climbing class off its `poi_r*` labels (`STYLE_CLIMBING_CLASS`), so no single route is labelled, the crags being the markers' (the maintainer, 2026-10-16, #734). The peak layer draws only features with a name or an elevation, because its source also carries cliffs and ridges as lines and most have neither, so they drew as peaks a click could not add. It registers the markers' two icons for `landmarks.ts`: `CLIMBING_IMAGE`, a climber cut out of a rock face, drawn on a canvas in the sprite's own disc frame at pixel ratio 2, and `TRAILHEAD_IMAGE`, a roofed signboard on two legs drawn the same way (the maintainer's pick, 2026-10-16); with no 2D canvas neither is registered, and the markers wear the sprite's `circle_stroked_11`. Record: [0159](../../../docs/decisions/0159-crags-destination-type.md) The rest: `glowTwin`, `poiLabelLayout` and `POI_LABEL_PAINT`, the specs it and the markers build from; `lakeAnchor`, where a clicked lake becomes a coordinate; the POI glow's canvas image; and `setSource`. Separate because none of them needs the component: each is handed a map, a canvas or an event. It imports MapLibre for its TYPES only, which is what lets `basemap.test.ts` run it in node against a stub map and pin the layer order `enhanceBasemap` adds, the filter it composes onto the style's water labels, and the `map-basemap-declares` and `map-basemap-layers` checks hold the file's allowed top-level functions and what it must say about the clickable peaks and lakes. **`src/map/` is inside every component check**: the ESLint class and hue bans take `src/map/**` beside `components/*.tsx`, and so do the per-file checks in `tools/eslint/checks/styles.js` and the source glob `styles.test.ts` keeps for its measured rules, because a folder of its own must not be a way out of the rules the component was under. The ESLint self-test lints one hue at a path under `map/` with the real config to prove the glob reaches it
- `src/map/basemapWatch.ts` — the basemap style's failure and its recovery (#580): `watchBasemap`, registered by `MapView` right after the map is built rather than through `mountFeatures`, because a style that fails never reaches `load`. An `error` before `style.load` that names no source is the style (`isStyleFailure` in `utils/basemapFailure.ts` decides, under node); it reports `onBasemapFailed(true)`, which App holds and the panel lists as `BASEMAP_FAILED_NOTE` in the Analyze button's messages, and asks for the style again with `setStyle(STYLE, { diff: false })` once per window `online` event while the failure is open, through `overlays/recovery.ts`. `style.load` clears both. No timer: OpenFreeMap sends no `Retry-After` to wait out
- `src/map/controller.ts` — the latest props for the handlers `MapView` registers once, on `load` (#410): draw mode, the displayed rows and the Windy inputs a result popup needs, the fire and closure warnings, the POI list and its two callbacks, the sheet's bottom padding for the framing calls inside the mount effect, and `onCameraMove`, where `map/camera.ts` sends the settled camera. A MapLibre handler closes over the first render's props for the session, and the component used to answer that with six mirror refs and four effects that did nothing but copy props into them. Now `MapView` holds one controller in `useState`, writes it in ONE effect declared before every other effect, and each handler reads `controller.inputs` when its event fires; a value copied out at registration is the stale closure the object exists to prevent, and the `map-shared-rules` check fails a destructure of it anywhere in the map's wiring. `resultAt`, `fireWarningAt` and `closureWarningAt` (#550) are the three reads the result popup makes. Separate because it is plain data with no map in it, so `controller.test.ts` can show in node that a handler made before an update sees the update
- `src/map/drawRing.ts` — the drawn ring on the map (#410): the `draw` source, the fill and outline, the midpoint and vertex handles (hidden outside draw mode, which is what stops a handle firing at all), the vertex drag by mouse or one finger, the midpoint insert that starts a drag on the new vertex, the remove-point popup, opened through the handles' click target in `mapPopups.ts` (first in the rank, and holding the grab cursor through a drag), and `commitRing`, the one way an edit reaches React: on a click, a drag's end, an insert or a removal, never mid-drag, so the URL is not rewritten on every pointer move. It does not own the points: the component hands it the holder, and it edits that through the pure `ringPolygon`, `moveVertex`, `removeVertex` and `insertOnSegment` in `utils/drawGeometry.ts`. At the ring cap (`maxPolygonPoints`, read from the controller at the moment a point would land, because `/api/capabilities` answers after the map mounts) a click places nothing and a midpoint grab is left to the map's pan, silently: the server refuses a longer ring (#619). Its colour, its handle sizes and its fill opacity are `map/mapStyles.ts`'s (#365); the remove-point button is `popupButton` from `utils/popup/popupChrome.ts`, opened with `openBare` because it is a button rather than a card

Hex values named for the Tailwind step they match, because the GL renderer takes a string and not a class; Tailwind v4's oklch steps rather than v3's since #687, so the label halo is the slate-900 the classes paint. Deliberately NOT the overlays' paint: each overlay's pure module under `utils/` feeds its layer and its legend swatch from one constant, and `basemap.ts` keeps its terrain-matched colours. The linter's `map-paint-named` check fails a bare paint number in any other `map/*.ts`, and `value-hex` fails a hex value anywhere under `src/` outside the owners `checks/styles.js` lists. - `src/map/terrain.ts` — the ground (#799): the shaded relief, the contour lines and the 3D terrain, all from Mapterhorn's elevation (`utils/terrainSources.ts`). Nothing is added on load: the first time one of the three switches on, `loadDemTiles` loads `maplibre-contour` with the module's one dynamic import, a chunk of its own, and registers its two schemes, and every elevation tile after that goes through its cache (`sharedDemProtocolUrl`), so a tile two layers need is fetched once. Read as `terrarium`, never MapLibre's default `mapbox`, which gives wrong heights without failing, and to zoom 12 (`DEM_MAXZOOM`, in `utils/terrainSources.ts`), the zoom Mapterhorn holds over all land. The relief (`relief`, a hillshade on `dem`) goes under the first `ofm-` layer or symbol and under the contours; the contours (`contours`, `contours-index`, and `contours-label` on the index lines) go over it at the same point, in feet on `CONTOUR_THRESHOLDS_FT`, from zoom 8 (`CONTOUR_MINZOOM`, the table's first entry, on the source and all three layers), so a region-wide view shows the big relief. The Layers row that switches them reads `Topographic`. A switch turned off hides its layers rather than removing them, and MapLibre asks for no tile of a source nothing visible uses. `opacity` takes the three rows' levels (record [0243](../../../docs/decisions/0243-layers-palette-and-base-map-opacity.md)): the slope's raster opacity is its level; the contours' lines and edge take their ink's opacity times the level over the default, held to 1, and their heights the same ratio; and the relief, which has no opacity, takes `reliefPaint`, the tuned look at the default, the shadow and light fading together under it and the depth and light doubling at 100. A layer at 0 hides as if switched off, and a layer added later is drawn at the level held. It owns its colours and paint numbers, which `value-hex` and `map-paint-named` list it for: one value each for both themes, because the map stays light in both. `terrain.test.ts` drives it with the stub map and a fake library.
